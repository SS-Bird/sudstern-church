Spaghet the Heck out of My Life 

Spaghetti and meatballs is that classic dish that makes you think of your mother’s cooking and comfort. ‘Tis a shame then that we have associated all that wonderfulness with the worst pasta choice possible! Good lord it's awful! I mean, a bowl of carbs covered in oily sauce is never going to taste bad, but it is definitively the worst way to format those carbs. 

I urge you to reject your complacency in pasta buying and actually analyze your choices. Just wait til you open your eyes to what a floundering mess spaghetti is. It has numerous, ruining failures. Let’s list 'em off, shall we?

Before you even get to the spaghetti you need an absolute goliath of a pot. Then you gotta portion the pasta, and it’s just so unnecessarily difficult. It makes you feel like goldilocks except it's impossible to find your “just right”. The next issue is spaghettis' lack of sauce gripping skill, you’d think it was actually designed to do the opposite. I ask spaghetti defenders, please point me in the direction of it’s nook and crannies necessary to hold something like a bolognese? There is a reason other pastas spected into traits like hollowness, concavity or a ridged surface, that's what holds sauce! Of course the meagre sauce it can retain will stain your lips, and you’ll probably stain your shirt too. You’ll inevitably wind up looking like a slob.

And now, spaghettis’ highest sin, what really ticks me off, and what started me on this crusade: it’s the nightmare that is spaghetti as leftovers. The starch around the pasta sticks each noodle together. This is not an issue for pastas with nice structural integrity, but as spaghetti traded that for… apparently nothing? This lack of strength results in your noodle breaking. Leaving you with inseparable clumps of pathetic, broken spaghetti.

Just stop putting up with your worst option! Why are you still dealing with spaghettis incompetence anyway? I’m betting it's a habit. What else does spaghetti have going for it? Just that it’s what you grew up eating. Just that it’s the default option. But when it’s your worst option maybe, yunno, don’t take it? Use your intelligent human mind and free will to rise above it’s commonplace failure. 

Step into an enlightened future with me. A future with cavatappi and meatballs.
