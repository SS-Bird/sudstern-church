# Scene 1 — The "hijacked ad" screen (shown when you click any car ad)

Clicking an ad opens a screen that looks like the ad has been hijacked by a communist hacker.
- `## Hijack`: `banner` (the warning line at the top), `link_text` / `link_url` (the link under the message),
  `close` (the button that goes back to the blog). The body is the hacker's message.
- `## Quote`: the Marx quote. `source` is the attribution under it; `cite: marx` awards the Marx sticker;
  `dictionary: consciousness` adds "consciousness" to Twerp's dictionary when the screen opens.
  More `## Quote` blocks would be shown in turn, one per ad click.

The quote wording is as Sophie gave it. Check it against the translation you cite (the 1859 Preface).
